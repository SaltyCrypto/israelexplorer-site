Privacy Policy

NearPast Israel Explorer — Last updated April 21, 2026

NearPast Israel Explorer ("we", "our", or "the app") is a location-based heritage discovery app for iOS. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Location Data

The app requests When In Use and optionally Always location access to power the History Radar feature, which alerts you when you are near a heritage site.

  • When In Use: Used to show nearby sites on the map and trigger proximity alerts while the app is open.
  • Always (optional): Used to deliver background alerts via significant-location-change monitoring when the app is in the background. You are asked to grant this separately; the app functions without it.
  • Your precise location is processed on-device only. We do not transmit your location to any server.

2. Motion & Activity Data

With your permission, the app reads motion activity (walking, driving, stationary) from the device's motion coprocessor. This data is used solely to improve alert relevance (e.g. suppressing alerts when stationary) and is never transmitted off-device.

3. Account Data

If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique user identifier, display name, and email address from those services. This information is stored securely in our Supabase backend and used only to:

  • Sync your visited sites and wishlist across devices.
  • Power social features (field notes, photos, leaderboard).

Sign-in is optional. You may use all core map and alert features without an account.

4. Usage Data

We log anonymized, aggregated analytics events (e.g. "paywall viewed", "alert fired") using an on-device counter. These counters are stored locally in UserDefaults and are not transmitted to any analytics service.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Backend for account sync and social features. Data stored in EU region. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In — Optional authentication. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Israel Antiquities Authority (IAA) — The Discover feature fetches public archaeological records from the IAA's open data API. No user data is sent to the IAA.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account. Local SwiftData (visited sites, wishlist) is deleted when you uninstall the app.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
